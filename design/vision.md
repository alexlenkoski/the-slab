# Vision

## High Concept

The player is the Warden of the Slab.

The Slab is the greatest prison in Pharloom, entrusted with capturing and preserving dangerous beings rather than destroying them.

The player leads expeditions into Pharloom, captures powerful creatures, maintains the prison, grows their workforce through the Brood Mother, and escorts prisoners to the Citadel where they are permanently preserved in the Memorium.

Every successful capture creates new dangers before eventually making the world safer.

---

## Core Fantasy

The player should feel like the commander of a living fortress.

Every expedition is a calculated risk.

Every captured prisoner increases both the prison's value and its danger.

Victory comes from maintaining order under constant pressure.

---

## Design Pillars

### Containment, not conquest

Enemies are valuable.

The objective is to imprison them rather than eliminate them.

---

### Every victory creates new problems

Capturing prisoners makes the prison more dangerous.

Escorting prisoners to the Citadel exposes them to rescue attempts.

Success always introduces new challenges.

---

### The Brood Mother is the heart of the Slab

The Brood Mother produces the workforce that allows the prison to function.

Protecting and feeding her is the foundation of every long-term strategy.

---

### Risk versus Security

Sending guards into Pharloom weakens the prison.

Keeping guards home limits expansion.

The player is constantly balancing growth against stability.
