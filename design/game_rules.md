# Victory

Protect the Brood Mother and successfully preserve three important prisoners from every major faction of Pharloom within the Memorium.

---

# Defeat

The game is lost if:

- The Brood Mother dies.
- The Slab suffers a catastrophic prison collapse.
- Too much of the Citadel is lost to continue the preservation project.

---

# Economy

Money is earned by completing Citadel contracts.

Money is spent on:

- Food
- Equipment
- Prison improvements
- Expedition improvements

---

# Population

The Brood Mother periodically produces new workers after being fed.

Workers may become:

- Guards
- Prison staff
- Expedition members

Population growth depends entirely on the health of the Brood Mother.

---

# Departments

Each Department studies one faction of Pharloom.

Each Department has:

- A Curator
- Contracts
- Research
- Memorium facilities

Each Department offers five important prisoners from its associated faction and
requires the player to choose and preserve three.

As captures progress, that faction launches increasingly dangerous attacks against the Department.

After all three prisoners have been successfully preserved, the faction's organized resistance collapses and attacks cease.

---

# Curators

Each Department is led by a single Curator.

The Curator:

- Issues contracts.
- Rewards successful expeditions.
- Oversees preservation.
- Unlocks research.

If a Curator dies:

- The Department permanently closes.
- All remaining contracts from that Department are lost.
- Research from that Department ends permanently.
- The Department becomes abandoned for the remainder of the game.

The death of a Curator is permanent.

---

# Factions

Every major faction has one opposing Department within the Citadel.

Factions only attack their corresponding Department.

As more prisoners are captured:

- attacks become larger
- tactics improve
- sieges become more frequent

This escalation represents growing faction organization and desperation.
Capturing optional prisoners reduces the forces available to take part in those
attacks, but does not prevent the campaign's escalation to more advanced
tactics.

The final preservation represents the climax of that faction's campaign.

# Contracts

Each Department offers five significant contracts associated with its faction.
The player chooses three of these targets to preserve.

One of the five is a mandatory target. The other four are choices, of which the
player selects two.

Ordinary contracts show:

- Payment
- Estimated danger
- Known abilities
- Expected retaliation
- Containment requirements
- Research reward

The mandatory contract becomes available after the first successful
preservation for its Department. It initially reveals only the target's
location. Capturing optional prisoners from that faction can gradually reveal
clues about the mandatory target, such as an attack trait, environmental hazard,
escort strength or containment need.

After the mandatory target and two other targets have been preserved, the
Department's remaining contracts close and the faction's organized resistance
collapses.

# Contract Deadlines and Trust

Contract deadlines continue to advance when an expedition withdraws or a target
escapes during transport.

When a contract expires:

- The contract fails and is later reissued.
- The Department's trust in the Warden decreases.
- All contracts from that Department pay less while trust is reduced.
- The faction has time to grow stronger.
- The target remains available, so an expired timer alone cannot permanently
  block campaign completion.

Successfully completing a contract restores some Department trust. Trust cannot
rise above the level at which that Department began the campaign.
